§ 04 · The awards

Two that matter.

Small numbers, big meaning. Both from bodies whose whole point is to name the people the field trusts.

2020 · Games for Change

Vanguard Award for Leadership

Presented at the Games for Change Festival

Peer honor from the DEI-and-social-impact body of the field. Gordon in his acceptance: "The work of games is the work of communities. Our contributions are additive to the nexus of ideas, explorations, and discourse which have defined Games for Change for the past seventeen years."

Pre-2019 · IGDA Black in Games

Jerry Lawson honor

From IGDA Black in Games, before the 2019 restructure

Named for Jerry Lawson, the Fairchild Channel F architect. Sometimes confused in the record with the IGDA Foundation's later annual Lawson Award. Different bodies, different years. Cierra McDonald was the inaugural recipient of the Foundation's version, in 2019.

§ 05 · The marginalia

Three items in the margins.

Not exactly public appearances, but part of the record if you are looking closely.

Six Feet Under · HBO · S3E3 · 2003

The walk-on he bought at Outfest

The Russian Roulette winnings bought a walk-on role at an Outfest charity auction. He plays a student in Claire's art class. A trapdoor on cable television, to a queer film festival, to HBO.

Cooking with Kay · Producer · 2007

Joe's film, Gordon's IMDb line

The unexplained 2007 producer credit on his IMDb headline is his husband Joe Heally's movie. Five festivals in its first year. Not a Gordon-fronted appearance, but part of why his filmography reads oddly if you don't know the household.

Speed Racer · Family film · 2022

Joe's 60th birthday tribute video

Brennan Murphy cut this for Joe's 60th on February 24, 2022. Gordon and Joe both on camera. "We love you, Speed Racer" is the line their son used, and it stuck. A private-facing artifact, publicly hosted.

USC · Since 2021

Anatomy of a Game and the classroom archive

The USC teaching record is its own body of appearances. Anatomy of a Game (2020) is the flagship, and a decade of guest lectures, ethics symposia, and student panels sit around it. Some are on YouTube. Most are inside SCA.

§ 06 · In his own words

What he actually says.

Every quote below is sourced. Nothing here is paraphrased.

"Games are a language that is shared everywhere in the world."

USC Today, Elisa Huang, March 16, 2021

"For marginalized people, a lot of energy is devoted to justifying your existence in spaces, so when you see yourself placed as default, it has real meaning."

High Score, Netflix, This Is War, 2020

"It's hard to be something if you can't see it. As I tell my students, many of the roles they'll have probably don't exist at the moment. All you can do is prepare to be the best version of yourself."

St. Paul's School alumni spotlight (class of '88)

"What I wanted was Maddens for everybody. How many ways can we have everyone at the table?"

St. Paul's School alumni spotlight

"I just remembered something. I also was the first person to put the Barcelona Dragons in Madden. It was me. I used to love European football."

Gamereactor, SDCC Málaga, November 8, 2025

"The Midwest has always been a quiet powerhouse in game development, where creativity, collaboration, and perseverance converge to shape the industry in profound ways."

MDEV 2024 keynote, Madison WI, November 7, 2024

Method

Every entry on this page is checked against a named publication, an official schedule, or a hosted recording. Where a date is uncertain, only the year is given. Where a recording is not publicly available, the entry says so. Nothing on this page is paraphrased from memory.

The Game Show chapter of his career lives on its own site. That is where the playable Killer Questions, the Taboo cards, and the money ladder are. It is a companion to this record, not a subset.

The counts at the top of the page are conservative floors. His teaching, mentoring, and undocumented industry room-work are not counted here; they are their own record.
