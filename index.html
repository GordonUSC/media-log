A concise introduction.

Gordon Bellamy is a games industry leader and Professor of the Practice of Cinematic Arts at USC. His career includes business and product roles at Electronic Arts, Tencent, and MTV, and executive leadership of the IGDA and the Academy of Interactive Arts & Sciences. He is CEO of Gay Gaming Professionals and has appeared in Netflix’s High Score and Prime Video’s It’s in the Game. He speaks about AI, creativity, leadership, and belonging.
