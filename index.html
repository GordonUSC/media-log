Method. Three source layers, in order of weight: surviving tape and recordings; published programmes, network graphics and named articles; and Gordon's own dated posts. On the colours. The three series were checked by computation, not by eye, for deuteranopia and protanopia against this exact background. Every pair clears the separation floor, and the legend is labelled so identity is never carried by colour alone.
