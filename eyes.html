Settings

Dwell means: rest your gaze on a button and it activates on its own after the time you choose. The button fills up so you can see it coming. Look away before it fills and nothing happens.

Dwell is off by default, because a TD Pilot with AssistiveTouch already sends its own taps and you do not want two systems firing at once. Turn it on only if your device does not click for you. 0.6 seconds is the navigation speed Joey uses. Everything here also works with a mouse, a touch screen, the Tab and arrow keys, or a switch.
