# Media site review: paid speaking and board opportunities

Prepared September 8, 2026. This is an editorial and product critique, not a measured conversion study.

## Finding
The previous site is a useful personal archive but asks the prospective buyer to infer the offer. Its first screen emphasizes the shape of the collection, not audience outcomes, availability or a next step. Board prospects receive no specific account of what Gordon could contribute.

## What changed
- Leads with Gordon's name, real USC portrait, a present-tense purpose and separate speaking/board paths.
- Places public recordings near the top. UConn video is opt-in with an external fallback; Microsoft fireside and Digital Civics links are distinct.
- Three proposed keynote briefs pair an audience with concrete discussion goals. These are offers to tailor, not claims that Gordon has already delivered newly titled talks.
- Paid speaking inquiries route to Gordon's existing Keppler profile. No fees or availability are invented.
- Board section focuses on product/audience, creative trust and talent/community. Wave service is presented with dated public evidence, not an unverified current tenure.
- Inquiry builder creates editable text locally. Board mailto opens a draft for the visitor; it sends nothing. Speaking route points to the bureau, not an unconnected form endpoint.
- Short bio copy and printable introduction include the selected theme or board perspective and current editable inquiry.
- Searchable media collection remains, with progressive disclosure, filters and source-status definitions. Historical archive and eye-gaze pages remain available.
- Mobile layout, keyboard tab navigation, reduced-motion support and visible focus states are included.

## Evidence and corrections
- Merged the two Carolinas Youth Mentoring Symposium entries: 50 inherited records become 49. Counts mean collection records, not total career appearances.
- MDEV record now uses 2025 and November 7–8, as in the organizer's event text. Its inconsistent 2024 announcement dateline is disclosed.
- Digital Civics now links to the July 9, 2025 episode rather than a Playmakers listing.
- The earlier Playmakers four-pillars title remains inherited and explicitly marked for confirmation; no unsupported replacement title is asserted.
- Restored the USC Today source link. Removed conflicting promotional totals from the main presentation.
- No audience reach, revenue lift, client results, board committee qualifications or invented testimonials have been added.

## Highest-value next evidence
1. A 60–90 second reel of actual paid-stage footage showing audience connection, a clear idea and a practical takeaway. Confirm footage permissions before editing or republishing.
2. Two or three attributable organizer quotes about a specific engagement and outcome. The verified MDEV announcement quote provides initial outside validation but is not a post-event satisfaction report.
3. A dated board CV with exact appointment dates, role types, committees and demonstrable contributions. This would support board recruiting better than documentary credits alone.
4. Align future fee positioning with Keppler. The public profile currently shows a “$10,000 and Below” guideline; a premium personal site does not change that buyer anchor by itself. Do not replace it with invented fees.
